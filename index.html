ABOUT
ME

I have always been drawn to the process of bringing ideas to life, from the earliest stages of exploration to the details that give a finished piece its identity. I find inspiration in observing the world, studying different forms of art, and discovering how seemingly unrelated ideas can come together to create something meaningful.

My approach to creative work is rooted in curiosity and experimentation. I enjoy exploring unfamiliar techniques, challenging my own perspectives, and finding solutions to creative problems. I believe that growth comes from remaining open to new ideas and treating every project as an opportunity to learn something beyond what I already know.

This portfolio serves as a reflection of that ongoing journey, documenting the things I have created, the ideas I have explored, and the skills I continue to develop.
